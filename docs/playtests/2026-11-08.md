# Playtest: story reveals, events, balance (2026-11-08)

What was run, and what it showed. Nobody hand-flew a fresh game end to end; flying was covered by the browser specs and
the economy was covered by a bot.

## What was run

* Unit tests: 947 passed.
* Browser suite (91 specs): 70 passed on the first full run. The other 21 failed while tests and source edits ran on the
  same machine (page reloads mid-test); on a quiet rerun 20 of them passed. The one real failure was a spec still
  expecting old wording, now fixed. One more spec needed the new research costs. All 91 now pass.
* Economy bot (`npm run bot`): ten game days on a seeded game with a fixed build order. It cannot fly: hauling is one
  120 kg trip every 150 s and the buoy and derelict finds are assumed at fixed times.
* Screenshots of a staged mid-game save: event chip, Journal, research tree with a story gate.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Severity | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alloy runs out first (about day 4.5) while wire and components pile up past 200 | balance | Seeded games get 1.4x iron on Selene: home-only play now reaches 15 to 16 techs instead of 12 before it needs other worlds |
| 2 | A brand-new game shows the parked lander drifting at 11 m/s until the first frame (the game opens paused) | bug | Fixed: the lander starts turning with the ground |
| 3 | Hauling clears all 31 starting rocks in about a day; after that the ship only earns from belts and comets | balance | Not changed. Suggest a slow drift of new rocks |
| 4 | Night is tight early: the store touches zero before dawn with three or four batteries | by design | Kept: it is the early power lesson. Flares reward batteries |
| 5 | Storms wreck a building every few days if nobody repairs | by design | Kept. Faults and Field repair give repairs a reason |
| 6 | The starting 28 alloy covers solar, refinery, battery, storage and a rig | fine | Kept: rigs only appear after industry runs, so the first sortie still matters |
| 7 | The Journal's "Recently" list sits inside a closed fold in the Objectives panel | minor UX | Not changed. Could open by default once there are entries |
| 8 | Research tree: story gate shows in the detail and on the start button ("Not yet: live through a storm first") | fine | — |
