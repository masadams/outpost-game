# Printed pilots and wrecks

**Story:** your pilots are printed bodies made at the base. Losing one is a setback, not the end.

## How it plays
- **Lose the lander** (crash, or destroyed in flight): press **Backspace** (or the button in the Ship panel) to print a new pilot and a basic lander on the pad for 8 alloy and 2 components. The new lander has a full hull, 60% fuel and no modules.
- **The old lander stays in the world** as a wreck, under the same physics: it lies where it crashed (turning with the ground), or keeps falling, orbits, or drifts away if it was lost in flight.
- **What survives** depends on how it was lost: a gentle crash keeps up to 90% of its cargo, fuel and some frame materials; a fast one as little as 25%; destroyed in flight, 60%.
- **Transponder:** for an hour the wreck shows on the Map and the radar with its state (on the ground, in orbit, falling, drifting away) and the time left. A red light blinks on it. After that it still exists, but you have to find it.
- **Salvage:** fly up and match its speed (within 40 m, under 5 m/s apart), or land beside it and walk over, then press **E**. Cargo, fuel, fitted modules and frame materials come back as far as your hold allows; come back for the rest. A wreck that drifts more than 60 km out is gone.
- **Lose only the pilot** (on foot): print a new one at the base for 2 alloy.
- Ore now only unloads at the pad (it used to unload wherever the lander sat).

## Known limits
- No new lander art for damage levels; a wreck uses the wrecked lander colours.
- Dead pilots leave nothing behind.
