# Growth run 4: outposts that live on their own, a reason for every world, and storms that keep coming

Follows `growth-run-3.md`. Same rules: small slices, a test for every rule, the browser specs for the area, docs, one merge per slice, depth before breadth, checked against the bible and roadmap first.

Where things stand after run 3: the first sortie is proven by a scripted pilot, power has distance, capacity, loss and islands, remote pads refuel, Helia is a deadly far place, storms and layouts follow the seed, and every building has an upgrade effect. What is thin: life on other worlds (domes only shield damage), a reason to go to Cinder beyond rare metals, what happens after the second storm, and moving outposts' goods without flying every load.
