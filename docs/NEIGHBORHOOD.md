## Navigation and interactions

Open **Nav** from the operations navigation, or inspect its persistent yellow guidance line above coast prediction. It shows range in metres, signed bearing, closing speed C and total relative speed ΔV. Bearing is from ship nose, or local up on foot. Positive closing means approaching; a sideways pass can have zero closing but high ΔV, so match total relative velocity. There is no autopilot, instant travel, fuel/range guarantee or guaranteed landing path. Home targets the existing green pad at rest.

The overview shows planet/atmosphere, live tagged rocks, home, ship and site rings. The radar and near-ship direction arrow retain selected-target orientation when the site is off screen. Far escaped objects can leave the fixed-scale overview; guidance still uses their actual position. A belt target follows the mass-weighted centroid of its remaining parents/fragments/ore; once empty it falls back to its associated physical site. Site targets follow their bodies. Select a **site** to interact rather than the whole belt centroid.

All physical bodies continue shared planet gravity/drag, with no far-field freeze or analytic marker substituted for the actual station. Sites exchange collision impulses with rocks/each other and sweep ship contacts; ship mining beams/combat projectiles can damage them. Gravity is generated by the planet, not mutual attraction between small bodies. Site dry hardware is 80 kg with an 8 m collision radius; ore/fuel adds to actual mass.

An intact site needs a flying pilot within **120 m**, **≤8 m/s total relative speed**, and no intervening planet. Press **E** once or use the native panel button. Holding E does not continually draw stock. Enter/Space activation stays out of ship mining/build controls. Pause allows destination selection/inspection and preserves it in K saves, but interactions and all movement stop.

- The Frost buoy surveys its belt without granting resources. The same survey accompanies a valid cache/tender visit, revealing the associated live manifest. Repeated visits do not duplicate flags or cues. Resource colors and ordinary nearest-rock telemetry still work before surveying.
- The derelict transfers up to its remaining **12 copper + 6 rare metals**, limited by real 120 kg hold room. Partial recovery leaves the balance at the site. Cargo mass increases ship inertia; material transfers at site velocity, conserving combined momentum. Return, land and unload normally; no direct depot credit.
- The tender transfers up to **18 kg fuel**, limited by the real tank capacity. Its mass falls as ship mass rises, with the same momentum accounting. It never regenerates fuel or repairs/rearms the ship.
- Wrecking a site releases its remaining cached raw ore once into normal tractor pickups, and vents any fuel into the loss counter. Dry hardware yields no new ore. A wreck remains a physical obstacle and cannot service/survey. Lost survey data and supplies are consequences, not respawn triggers.

The short objective suggests Frost survey → derelict recovery → normal home ore delivery. Recovery plus a subsequent successful depot delivery marks completion; it awards no additional material. Destroying an unrecovered cache changes the objective to debris recovery/other exploration, rather than presenting an impossible intact rendezvous. Directly tractored wreck debris is ordinary ore and does not count as an intact-cache recovery.

## Persistence and budgets

Version 11 saves belt origin on fragments/ore, all site bodies/stocks/damage, selection, surveys, fuel loss and objective progress. Loading and revisiting never seed content. On versions 1–10, old supported objects/resources stay intact and new uniquely identified sectors are appended once, after v1 planetary reprojection. No already-mined starter ID is recreated. If an extreme imported save has more than 244 existing parents, migration adds sites without overflowing the 256-rock loader bound; ordinary historic runs are well below that limit. Existing checkpoints already carrying this extension are not seeded twice.

The fresh content can produce **54 fragments** or **216 drifting ore stacks** from its 27 parents; the derelict can add at most two stacks. These are alternative material states, not duplicated populations. Site count stays three even when wrecked. The existing decoder bounds remain 256 rocks/256 pickups and existing threat/effect caps remain. All active physics advances at 60 Hz independently of rendering. No LOD freeze is needed at this bounded size.

`npm run benchmark` measures the pure shared simulation separately from rendering. In the managed container, Vitest 3.2.7 measured means of **0.0355 ms** (27 parents + 3 sites), **0.0968 ms** (54 fragments + 3 sites) and **0.0820 ms** (216 ore + 3 sites) per step, with p99 0.156 / 0.278 / 0.320 ms. Hardware, JIT and garbage collection affect timing; this is a physics budget measurement, not a browser frame-rate claim. The 16.7 ms frame budget must also cover Phaser/DOM/rendering. Reproduce with the checked-in benchmark rather than enforcing brittle timing tests.

## Playtest

1. K saves. Open Nav and select **Frost survey buoy**. P pauses for chart/zoom inspection; target selection still works. Confirm home-return and range/bearing/ΔV survive K/reload paused.
2. Launch while clearing/avoiding the starter sample. Reach space, build orbital speed and steer toward the selected marker; use the actual C/ΔV values to brake relative to the buoy. Avoid hitting its physical hull. No autopilot is provided.
3. At ≤120 m and ≤8 m/s, E surveys. Frost arc's live manifest replaces “unsurveyed.” Mine/tractor/tow its actual finite bodies; the manifest follows fragmentation and collection.
4. Select **Copper derelict**, rendezvous and E to recover up to 18 kg. A full hold leaves stock behind. Repeating E cannot duplicate ore. Save/reload to verify depletion.
5. Select **Return to home pad**, land using the existing warning lights and unload. Intact salvage recovery followed by a successful delivery confirms the return objective. Depot storage constraints still apply.
6. With tank room, rendezvous with **Far rim fuel tender**. E takes only missing fuel up to its remaining 18 kg. Full tank/repeated visits cannot create stock. Try a failed fast approach or a site wreck on a disposable save.
7. Verify Nav, guidance, region, coast path and instruments at 900×600, 1280×800 and 1920×1080. Supply damage, survey sounds and pause/mute behavior should remain readable and unobtrusive.
